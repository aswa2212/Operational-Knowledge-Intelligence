# Pricing and Discount Exception Policy — v2.0

## Discount Approval Thresholds
- Discounts up to 10% (discount_percent <= 10): Standard sales representative approval (auto-actionable).
- Discounts between 11% and 20% (discount_percent > 10 and discount_percent <= 20): Requires Sales Manager approval.
- Discounts exceeding 20% (discount_percent > 20): Requires Director or VP of Sales approval (requires_approval).

## Deal Size Rules
- Standard deals under $25,000 follow standard tier pricing.
- Enterprise deals over $50,000 require custom quote generation and finance review.
